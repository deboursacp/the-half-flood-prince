Double-overhead waves, howling onshore wind, and that raw Pacific energy — it's the kind of session that reminds you why you got into this sport. But storm sessions at Half Moon Bay are not the place to wing it (pun intended).

- **Helmet:** Non-negotiable in waves
- **Impact vest:** Highly recommended
- **5/4 wetsuit:** Water temps are 50–55°F year-round. Booties and gloves in winter.
- **Safety leash:** Use a kite leash that allows full flagging of your kite
- **Bright colors:** Visibility matters when conditions are gnarly

## Final Thoughts

Crissy Field is the Bay Area's playground. Half Moon Bay is its proving ground. Storm sessions here will push you, humble you, and — if you do it right — reward you with some of the best rides of your life.

But the ocean doesn't care about your Instagram edit. Respect the conditions, respect the community, and come home safe.

See you out there — but only if the forecast is right. 🌊🪁
