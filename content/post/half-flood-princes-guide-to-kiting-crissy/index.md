There's a reason Crissy Field is the most famous kite spot in the Bay Area. Nestled between the Golden Gate Bridge and the Marina District, it offers consistent thermal winds from April through October, flat-water conditions on the right tide, and a view that makes every session feel like a postcard.

<!--more-->

But Crissy has a learning curve — and it's not just about your kite skills. Timing is everything here. Get the tide wrong and you're either body-dragging through ankle-deep mud or fighting chop that turns your board into a jackhammer. This guide breaks down how to read the conditions and pick your window like a local.
