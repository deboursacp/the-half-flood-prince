Welcome to The Half Flood Prince — a resource for kiteboarding in the San Francisco Bay Area.

This site covers spot guides, etiquette, and general tips that I've picked up over the years. 

### Who's behind this?

@petey_kites - Just a local kiter who's spent too many hours staring at wind forecasts and wants to share what I've learned. This site is a labor of love — no ads, no sponsors, just stoke.
