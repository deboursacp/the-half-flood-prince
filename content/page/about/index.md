Welcome to The Half Flood Prince — a resource for kiteboarding in the San Francisco Bay Area.

Generally this content skews intermediate to adavanced (focusing on expert sites / conditions).

This site covers spot guides, etiquette, and general tips that I've picked up over the years. 

### Who's behind this?

@petey_kites - Just a local kiter who's spent too many hours staring at wind forecasts and wants to share what I've learned. This site is a labor of love — no ads, no sponsors, just stoke.

### Contact

Have a question or want to contribute? Reach out on [instagram @petey_kites](https://www.instagram.com/petey_kites).


### Contributions

I welcome everything from corrections to entire posts. If you want to submit pull requests, feel free to at [github](https://github.com/deboursacp/the-half-flood-prince/pulls) or DM me on instagram to get in touch and I can work with other formats.
